# Customer CRM — staff playbook

## Purpose
Use the admin CRM as the shop's shared record of sales leads, existing customers, quotations and after-sales follow-up. It is not a payment processor or a substitute for accounting records.

## Daily workflow
1. Add each genuine enquiry or customer once. Search by name, phone, email or company before creating a duplicate.
2. Capture the customer's preferred contact method, the product(s) they are interested in, enquiry source and the staff owner.
3. Set a clear next follow-up date whenever a quotation, stock confirmation or customer decision is pending.
4. After each call, WhatsApp, email, visit, complaint or sale, log a short factual activity summary and outcome.
5. Update the lifecycle stage: Lead → Prospect → Customer → Repeat Customer. Use Inactive only when follow-up is no longer appropriate.
6. Review the Follow-ups Due count at the start of each shift; contact overdue customers and update the next action.
7. Keep notes professional and relevant. Never store passwords, card details, bank credentials, or unnecessary sensitive personal data.

## Roles
- **Super Admin / Manager:** view and manage CRM contacts, assign staff, review the pipeline.
- **Order Staff:** add and update customer records, assign follow-ups, log interactions.
- **Inventory Staff:** no access to customer records; focus on product drafts and stock movements.

## Recommended service standard
- New WhatsApp/phone enquiries: acknowledge promptly during business hours.
- Quotes sent: set a follow-up within 1 business day unless the customer agrees otherwise.
- Out-of-stock requests: log the requested product and a date to update the customer.
- Complaints and returns: log the issue, action owner, and promised response date; keep customer communication factual.
- Delivered orders: record confirmation and any warranty/after-sales action.

## Data quality
Use Nigerian phone format consistently (for example, +234…). Keep one primary record per customer and update it instead of creating duplicates. Use the company/source fields for business customers and referrals. Do not mark a lead as a customer until a purchase is confirmed.

## Current limits
The CRM currently supports contact records, assignment, lifecycle stage, follow-up dates and an activity timeline. It does not yet sync automatically with orders, WhatsApp messages, email, Paystack, or quotation documents. Those integrations should be added only after the corresponding workflows are implemented and tested.
